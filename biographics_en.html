🦁 LORE AND BIOGRAPHY

✦ GENERAL INFORMATION

Name: Rometto.

Race: Feral half-demon lion.

Current Status: Wanderer, finding peace in a new home.

Features: Height 417 cm, weight from 9.2 to 15 tons. Colossal, powerful build (paw width 70 cm). Sclerae are clear (they turn black only during the manifestation of his demonic form).

✦ BLOOD AND MEMORY: MECHANICS OF IMMORTALITY

Rometto possesses a unique gift of rebirth thanks to the blood of his demon father. After every death, he returns to the world as a small lion cub in a random location. His growth is accelerated.

During adolescence, his memory begins to return. This process occurs in flashes and is sometimes incredibly painful — like a sudden high-voltage electric shock. This forces him to relive past tragedies over and over again, and to search anew for his path and those dear to him. Currently, Rometto is living his 19th life. It is in this incarnation that his body has reached its true grandeur and colossal size naturally, becoming the absolute peak of his physical development.

✦ LIFE STORY
1. Childhood Tragedy and the Legacy of Shamans

Rometto was born in harsh outland territories ruled by hyenas. His mother was the lioness Lily, and his father was the cruel demon Agares. Discovering a spark of his power in his son, Agares tried to destroy him by throwing him off a cliff into a gorge. Rometto survived thanks to the intervention of shamans from an ancient cult, who became his new family and taught him magic. Later, Rometto clashed with his father in combat. He managed to trap his father in a stone, which later disappeared without a trace. The price of victory was a severe injury to his right paw.

2. The Golden Age and the Fall of Dalor

In the great empire of Dalor, Rometto became a legend. As the right hand of the great kings Jax, Munol, and Augustus I, he gained fame as a fearless warrior and wise shaman. Since childhood, Rometto believed in the legend of the Golden Lion, but that lion had long since died. In this pride, he was the first to bring in a hyena — his loyal friend Charlie. However, betrayals led to the death of his friends. Due to a foreign will that enslaved his demonic power, Rometto was cursed, and his eyes turned black. After the fall of the empire, in a fit of rage, he burned its ruins to the ground.

3. The War with the Gods and the Burden of the Past

In his 16th reincarnation, Rometto founded his own pride, Reverse Time, but the gods, fearing his power, bound him in celestial chains. For years, he watched the suffering of his children from the heavens, until his rage gave him the strength to break the shackles. Rometto declared war on the heavens and crushed many deities. This tragedy left a mark: his daughter is alive. She carries his blood and acknowledges him, but constantly reproaches her father for not being there in her time of need.

✦ INNER CIRCLE AND FRIENDS

Despite his past loneliness, Rometto surrounded himself with loyal allies:

  • Crystalna: The only lioness with whom he shares a strong, pure friendship. They had a sexual relationship in the past, but remained best friends. Both firmly believe that true friendship cannot be ruined by sex.
  • Walter: A lion who became almost like a blood brother to Rometto.
  • Richard: Rometto's adopted son.
  • Phil and Bess: Loyal friends, a lion and lioness, always ready to support him.
  • Batet: A lioness friend who is currently considered missing.
✦ BIOLOGY AND HUNTING: THE PHYSICS OF EXTERMINATION
I. INFRASONIC ROAR

Generates waves below 10 Hz. The resonance causes paralysis of will and sheer terror in victims within a 50-meter radius.

II. INERTIAL CHARGE

With a mass of 9.2 tons, he reaches 50 km/h. Any obstacle (trees, armor) simply deforms.

III. GRAVITATIONAL CRUSH

Landing generates a force of 30–40 tons. Rometto simply shifts his weight onto a front paw 70 cm wide.

The bite force of 24,000 PSI is used extremely rarely due to the inconvenience of his dimensions.

✦ A NEW CHAPTER: EVERYDAY LIFE AND THE POWER OF LOVE

Guardian Birds: Serve as his eyes and ears. They gained immortality by drinking his demonic blood.

Living Heater: All pride members sleep together with him in one cave, using him as a giant living heater.

Healing: Upon meeting a lioness with a beautiful red coat and truly falling in love with her, Rometto found healing. His old injury vanished: he simply stood up one day and realized that nothing hurt anymore.
