LORE AND BIOGRAPHY

✦ GENERAL INFORMATION

Name: Rometto.

Race: Feral half-demon lion.

Status: Wanderer, finding peace in a new home.

Features: Height 417 cm, weight 9.2-15 tons. Paw width 70 cm. Black sclerae only in demonic form.

✦ BLOOD AND MEMORY

Rometto possesses a unique gift of rebirth. After every death, he returns as a small lion cub in a random location. Currently, he is living his 19th life.

Memory returns in painful flashes during adolescence, forcing him to relive past tragedies and search anew for his path and loved ones.

✦ LIFE STORY
1. Childhood Tragedy

Born in territories ruled by hyenas. His mother was Lily, his father the demon Agares. Agares tried to kill him, but Rometto was saved and raised by ancient shamans who taught him magic. He eventually defeated his father, trapping him in stone.

2. The Empire of Dalor

As the right hand of great kings, he became a legendary warrior and shaman. Believing in the legend of the Golden Lion, he built his own legacy. After betrayals led to the death of his friends and the fall of the empire, Rometto burned its ruins to the ground in a fit of rage.

3. War with the Heavens

In his 16th life, the gods bound him in celestial chains out of fear. His rage allowed him to break free and declare war on the heavens. His daughter survives today but reproaches him for his absence during her darkest hours.
