Bio-Archive Rometto: About the Project

This project is an interactive digital archive created to systematize a massive amount of data about Rometto — a giant half-demon lion with a kind heart and a shoulder height of 417 cm.

🎯 Why is this needed?

Rometto is a character with deep lore, a complex history, and detailed physiology. Keeping all the variables in mind became physically difficult, and the number of questions from the community grew daily. This resource solves three key tasks:

  • External Memory: Protecting data from loss and confusion. Every centimeter and every kilogram of the character is recorded here.
  • Scientific Approach: All parameters are calculated based on physics and biological scaling laws. This is not just a fantasy, but a mathematical model of a giant.
  • Ultimate Reference: A ready-made tool for artists and roleplayers. No more "approximately" — only accurate, canonical data.

🔞 Addressing Specific Questions

During community discussions, questions often arose regarding the details of the reproductive system, metabolism, and more. I took this seriously. Instead of ignoring these requests, I performed calculations based on a body mass of 9.2 tons. These figures are now an official part of the canon.
