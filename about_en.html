Rometto Archive: About the Project

This project is an interactive digital archive created to systematize a colossal amount of data regarding Rometto, a giant demon-lion weighing over 9 tons.

🎯 Why is this needed?

Rometto is a character with deep lore, complex history, and detailed physiology. This resource solves three key tasks:

  • External Memory: Protecting data from loss and confusion. Every centimeter and every kilogram of the character is recorded here.
  • Scientific Approach: All parameters are calculated based on physics and biological scaling coefficients. This is a mathematical model of a giant.
  • Ultimate Reference: A tool for artists and role-players. Only precise, canonical data.

🔞 Addressing Specific Questions

During community discussions, questions often arose about the details of the reproductive system and metabolism. I approached this seriously. Instead of ignoring requests, I performed calculations based on a body mass of 9.2 tons. Now, this data is an official part of the canon.

🚀 Future Plans

  • Creation of a SFW version of the sheet.
  • Full English localization.
  • Support for other characters in the universe.
