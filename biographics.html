LORE AND BIOGRAPHY

✦ GENERAL INFORMATION

Name: Rometto.

Race: Feral Half-Demon Lion.

Current Status: Wanderer seeking a new place to settle for a long time.

Physical Traits: Height 417 cm, weight from 9.2 to 15 tons. Giant, powerful build (paw width 70 cm). Eye whites are clear (they turn black only during the manifestation of his demonic form).

✦ LIFE STORY
1. Childhood Tragedy and Shamanic Heritage

Since childhood, Rometto was curious, often seeking adventures alone and sometimes unintentionally putting himself in danger, unaware of the risks in foreign lands. His mother was an ordinary lioness named Lily who survived violence, and his father was the cruel demon Agares. When the father learned the son inherited some of his abilities, he tried to destroy him by throwing him off the cliffs into a gorge. The attempt failed, and Rometto was saved by shamans of an ancient forgotten cult, who became his new family and helped develop his demonic powers. Later, Rometto attempted revenge and fought his father. Alas, his mother died in that battle. He himself was wounded and limped on his right paw. He managed to seal his father into a stone, which later disappeared without a trace. Currently, his father's fate is unknown.

2. Golden Age and the Fall of Dalor

In his teenage years, Rometto noticed he was taller and larger than others, for which he was often called a mutant. As an adult, his massive size allowed him to stand out and take a prominent place in the pride. In the great empire of Dalor, he became a legend. For a long time, he was a wise shaman and advisor to many pride kings (Jax, Munol, and August I). Since childhood, Rometto believed in the legend of the Golden Lion, but that lion had died long ago. He was the first to bring a hyena—his loyal friend Charlie—into this pride. However, one of the kindest kings was killed due to Rometto's deception. This tragedy made him more cautious, although he remains kind at heart. Later, the lionesses who came to power stripped him of his influence and left him with nothing. Due to a foreign will enslaving his demonic power, Rometto was cursed, and his eyes turned black. After the empire's fall, he burned its ruins to the ground in a fit of rage.

3. War with the Gods and the Burden of the Past

In his 16th reincarnation, Rometto founded his own pride, Reverse Time, but the gods, fearing his power, shackled him in celestial chains. For years, he watched the suffering of his children from the heavens until rage gave him the strength to break the bonds. Rometto declared war on the heavens and crushed many deities. This tragedy left a mark: his daughter is alive. She carries his blood and recognizes him but constantly reproaches her father for not being there in her time of need. Now all his friends have parted ways, and Rometto remains a wanderer, traveling from place to place in search of a new home.

✦ CHARACTER AND PERSONALITY

By nature, Rometto is a curious, adventurous, and very strong-willed lion. Despite the hardships he has endured, he remains kind, though past experiences have made him much more cautious and sometimes melancholic. However, he always strives to appear friendly.

Due to past trauma and short, unsatisfactory relationships, he has completely lost trust in lionesses. Nevertheless, he maintains deep devotion, loyalty, and hope toward his true friends. He has a best friend whom he meets periodically, and this bond is very important to him.

✦ ORIENTATION AND FEELINGS

Rometto does not trust females due to past experience; most, in his opinion (which is high), seek either sex or power. He does not want to be an object for the satisfaction of others' desires or just another "seed bank." He is attracted to males, likely due to past trauma and a lack of love from his demon father.

In relationships, he seeks not so much sexual proximity—which is difficult to realize due to his size—but deep romance and warmth: sincere hugs, kisses, spending pleasant time together, quiet night walks, and true soul intimacy with a truly close being.

✦ INNER CIRCLE AND FRIENDS

Both are firmly convinced that true friendship cannot be ruined by sex. Although now friendly sex is possible only in controlled dreams or by other means.
  • Walter: A lion who has become almost like a blood brother to Rometto.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Does Rometto have a growth limit? ▼
Yes, 417 cm is his current limit and absolute peak of physical development in this life.
What magical abilities has he retained? ▼
Most are lost (they might only break through during extreme stress or fear). One of the few remaining on a permanent basis is mind reading, but he doesn't use it on principle. He can also communicate with spirits.
Can he be defeated? ▼
Yes, he can. But it is a secret known only to three living beings in all worlds.
Who is the red lioness who healed Rometto? ▼
It's a secret for now. Rometto met her in his new pride. Her sincere love became the catalyst for healing—the old injury to his right paw disappeared on its own.
What happened to Agares after being sealed in the stone? ▼
He is either dead or alive... but he hasn't appeared for a very long time. Most likely missing or simply temporarily weakened. (His fate is one of those mysteries that might unexpectedly return to the plot 😉)
How exactly does Rometto's reincarnation work? ▼
After death, he appears as a small cub in a random place in the world, born to a random pregnant lioness. The demonic essence in his soul infects the blood again. Growth is accelerated. Memories return in flashes during his teenage years and often cause severe pain. He is currently living his 19th life—the most perfect in terms of physical form.
What is the Reverse Time pride and does it exist now? ▼
Reverse Time was founded by Rometto in his 16th life. After the war with the gods, the pride disbanded. Some of its members survived both the collapse of the pride and Rometto's reincarnation. Among them are Bess and Crystallina. In his 19th life, Rometto is gradually building a new home and a new family.
